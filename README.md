# Articulated

[Articulated](https://articulated.app/) is an AI speech-coaching app for articulation training, public speaking, and professional communication. Practice interview answers, meeting updates, presentations, and everyday conversations, then review feedback on clarity, fluency, structure, vocabulary, confidence, and engagement.

Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760625926) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kugna.articulated).

## Speaking practice resources

- **[Free articulation and speaking practice](https://rick2781.github.io/speaking-practice-prompts/)** — 30 original prompts, a practice timer, and exercises for clearer answers.
- **[Speaking practice library](https://github.com/rick2781/speaking-practice-prompts)** — reusable prompts and a seven-day communication practice plan.
- **[Articulation training for professionals](https://articulated.app/blog/articulation-training-for-professionals)** — guidance for meetings, presentations, and workplace conversations.
- **[30-day articulation training plan for adults](https://articulated.app/blog/30-day-articulation-training-plan-for-adults)** — a structured plan for independent practice.

The public practice library is free and works without an account. Personalized AI coaching in the Articulated app requires a paid plan after any eligible trial.
